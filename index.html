Help & FAQ

Common questions about DermaNova AI. Click a question to expand the answer.

What does DermaNova AI do?

DermaNova AI analyses skin-lesion images using a deep-learning model (EfficientNetV2) and estimates the probability that a lesion is malignant. It also provides an AI-generated plain-language explanation of the result.

How do I run a scan?

Click the + button next to the message box, choose Upload files to select a photo or Take photo to use your camera. The model then processes the image and returns a result card.

What do the Dashboard numbers mean?

Total Scans is the number of image analyses you've run. AI Avg Confidence is the average certainty of the model across those scans. High Risk Flags counts lesions the model classified as malignant, and Total Chats is your number of saved conversations.

Is the result a medical diagnosis?

No. DermaNova AI is a decision-support tool. Results must always be confirmed by a qualified dermatologist through dermatoscopy and, where indicated, biopsy.

How do I update my profile?

Open the user menu at the bottom-left of the sidebar and choose Profile. You can change your display name and account email there.

Where is my chat history stored?

Conversations are saved locally in your browser. Use the Share Chat option in the top bar to send a conversation to someone else.
